## Design rules applied everywhere

- One typographic scale: 8 pt ticks, 9 pt labels, 10 pt bold panel letters `(a)`, no
  figure-level mega-titles (captions live in LaTeX, not in the PNG).
- Colorblind-safe palettes: `viridis` for magnitudes, `RdBu` diverging (blue = ML
  improves, red = degrades) for all skill/gain fields — replaces the previous
  red–yellow–green.
- Global maps in Robinson projection, land-masked; regional event maps in
  PlateCarree with coastlines/borders.
- Skill heatmap cells annotated with skill % (+ absolute model score beneath),
  text color auto-switched for contrast.
- Missing evaluation products render as clearly labeled "pending" panels, never
  silent failures — the set always regenerates end-to-end.

## Fig 1 rationale (what changed and why)

The previous Fig 1 was six text boxes with arrows — informationally correct but
visually flat and redundant with the prose. The redesign communicates the two
claims that make the paper distinctive *pictorially*:

1. **The prior is physical, not white.** Two rendered noise fields (isotropic
   Gaussian vs spatially correlated EOF-LHS) sit side by side, with the blend
   equation ρ·EOF + √(1−ρ²)·rand between them. The visual contrast between
   white noise and coherent large-scale structure *is* the argument.
2. **Sampling is an ODE from that prior.** A small trajectory inset shows member
   paths integrating from scattered x₀ to the target distribution over t∈[0,1]
   with Euler-step markers, labeled dx/dt = v_θ(x_t, c, t, ℓ).

Input/generator/output stages are compact cards with a mini U-Net glyph
(encoder–bottleneck–decoder with skip arcs and a cross-attention node) instead of
paragraph-length box text. A numbered ①–④ reading order and a single footer line
with the headline numbers replace the old floating annotation.
